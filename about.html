Work About

Tiffany is a branding designer of 13 years turned UI/UX designer.

Her experience ranges from agency to corporate settings within a wide array of industries. She has contributed design work to local and national brands, some including, Marriott Hotels, Costco Auto Program, and Breakfast Republic.

Guided by a never-ending curiosity and a hunger for the details, Tiffany’s work centers around design systems and interactions with a close attention to visual identity.

BTS: I am a NYC native who chased the California sun to San Diego 13 years ago. I’m currently based out of Orange County, CA. Some of my interests include: my dog, your dog, good food/coffee/matcha, music festivals, disc golf, a solid arts & crafts sesh, and traveling (and then missing my dog).
